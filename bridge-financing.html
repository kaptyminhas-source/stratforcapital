What Is Bridge Financing?

Bridge financing is a short-term mortgage that "bridges" the gap between two events — most often when you're buying a new property before an existing one sells, or when you need fast capital to secure a deal that conventional lenders can't fund in time. Secured by Alberta real estate, a bridge loan gives you immediate liquidity for a defined period, then is repaid when your exit event occurs: a sale, a refinance, or longer-term financing coming into place.

As an Alberta private lender, Stratfor Capital underwrites on the property and your exit strategy rather than rigid bank criteria — which means a clear plan and sufficient equity can get funded in days, not weeks.

When Bridge Financing Makes Sense

Buy/Sell Gaps

Purchase your next property before your current one closes, then repay the bridge from the sale proceeds.

Time-Sensitive Acquisitions

Close on a discounted or off-market deal quickly when a conventional approval would take too long.

Repositioning & Value-Add

Fund renovations or stabilize an income property to qualify for permanent financing or a higher sale price.

Refinance in Progress

Cover a maturing loan or payout while a new mortgage or sale is being finalized.

Equity Take-Out

Unlock equity in an existing Alberta property to fund a deposit, a partner buyout, or a new project.

Business Cash Flow

Bridge a short-term capital need for a real-estate-backed business while a longer-term solution is arranged.
