Privacy Policy

Stratfor Capital · Edmonton, Alberta · Last updated July 2026

Stratfor Capital ("we," "us," "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through stratforcapital.com, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

When you submit our contact form, we collect the information you provide: your name, email address, phone number, property location, estimated loan amount, and project description. We do not require you to create an account or provide financial account numbers, SIN, or payment details through this website.

Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) automatically logs standard technical information for security and performance purposes, such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your lending inquiry and assess whether we can offer a financing solution.
  • To contact you by phone, email, or text about your inquiry.
  • To maintain records related to loan applications and, if applicable, funded loans.
  • To improve and secure our website.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

How We Share Information

We may share information with:

  • Service providers who help us operate our business — for example, our email delivery provider and website host — solely to perform services on our behalf.
  • Legal counsel involved in facilitating and disbursing funds for a loan, where applicable.
  • Regulators or authorities where required by law.

Data Retention

We retain inquiry and loan-related information for as long as needed to respond to your request, service any resulting loan, and meet our legal and record-keeping obligations.

Your Rights

Under Alberta's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA), you may request access to, or correction of, the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to delete information we are not required to retain. To make a request, email info@stratforcapital.com.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information you submit. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Cookies

This site does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Our hosting provider may use minimal technical cookies/logs necessary to deliver and secure the site.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy can be directed to info@stratforcapital.com or 780-466-1203.
