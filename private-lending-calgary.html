Frequently Asked Questions — Private Lending in Calgary

How fast can I get a private mortgage in Calgary?

We issue term sheets within 24–48 hours of reviewing your deal, and funds are typically disbursed through legal counsel within 5–7 business days.

What interest rates do private lenders charge in Calgary?

Our rates range from 8% to 16% depending on the property, loan-to-value, position (1st or 2nd mortgage), and exit strategy. Bridge loans typically run 8–14%; construction loans 10–16%.

Can I qualify with bad credit or as a self-employed borrower?

Yes. We underwrite primarily on the property's equity and your exit plan. Credit history and income documentation matter less than they do at a bank.

What can a private loan be used for?

Buy/sell bridge gaps, time-sensitive acquisitions, construction and renovation, equity take-outs, partner buyouts, refinancing a maturing loan, and real-estate-backed business cash flow.

Do you lend outside Calgary?

Yes — we lend Alberta-wide, including Edmonton, Red Deer, Lethbridge, and smaller communities.
