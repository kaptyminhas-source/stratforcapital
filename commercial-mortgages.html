Private Commercial Lending, Built Around the Deal

Conventional commercial mortgages are slow and rigid — they lean heavily on covenants, lengthy underwriting, and perfect borrower profiles. As an Alberta private lender, Stratfor Capital underwrites primarily on the property, its income, and the strength of your exit. That makes us a fit for investors and developers who need speed, flexible structure, or a solution outside the bank box.

We lend against commercial and investment real estate throughout Alberta, structuring 1st and 2nd mortgages up to $5M+ with terms aligned to your business plan, whether that's acquisition, refinance, repositioning, or an equity take-out.
