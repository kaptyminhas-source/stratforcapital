Frequently Asked Questions — Private Lending in Edmonton

How fast can I get a private mortgage in Edmonton?

Term sheets within 24–48 hours; funding typically in 5–7 business days through legal counsel.

What does a private mortgage cost in Edmonton?

Rates range from 8% to 16% depending on property, loan-to-value (up to 75%), mortgage position, and exit strategy. All costs are disclosed in the term sheet before you commit.

Do you fund second mortgages?

Yes — we take 1st or 2nd mortgage positions on Alberta real estate, subject to combined LTV limits.

Can I get financing if the banks turned me down?

Often, yes. Bank declines are frequently about documentation, timelines, or property type rather than deal quality. We evaluate the equity and the exit.

What areas around Edmonton do you serve?

The full metro — St. Albert, Sherwood Park, Spruce Grove, Leduc, Fort Saskatchewan, Beaumont — and Alberta-wide beyond that.
